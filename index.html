About

I sit at the point where design and code stop being two different jobs. I build the interface and I design what gets people to click on it in the first place — the site, the thumbnail, the poster, the banner.

On the build side that means HTML, CSS and JavaScript, animated with GSAP, wired up with tools like EmailJS when a project needs a working contact form without a backend. On the design side it means posters, banners and YouTube / Facebook thumbnails built to be understood — and clicked — in about three seconds.
